## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Post-foundation melee combat scope

After foundation MVP, the project SHALL support a minimal melee combat loop as the first gameplay-depth expansion.

#### Scenario: Combat loop defined

- **WHEN** melee combat change is evaluated
- **THEN** player MUST be able to attack enemies, enemies MUST patrol and deal contact damage, and enemies MUST die from player hits

#### Scenario: Combat remains pet-project scoped

- **WHEN** combat features are proposed beyond melee-combat change
- **THEN** bosses, combo systems, and complex AI MUST remain out of scope unless a new change explicitly adds them
